Phone alerts: make the key pair

This page makes a fresh secret key pair inside your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Do not share the private key with anyone, including Claude.

1. VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY

2. VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY (secret)

3. VAPID_SUBJECT

mailto:ali@bashkaexpress.com
Add all three in Vercel: project bashka → Settings → Environment Variables (Production). Then redeploy. After that, close this page. If you lose the private key just open this page again and make a new pair (drivers will tap "Phone alerts" once more).
